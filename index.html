1. Information We Collect & Store

A. Local Game Progress & Player Profile Data (Stored On Your Device)

Water Sort Master stores your gameplay progress locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences storage. This includes:

  • Your chosen display nickname and royal avatar emblem entered on the welcome screen.
  • Current puzzle level, completed levels, level score points, and auto-saved mid-game bottle positions.
  • Virtual currency balances (Coins 🪙), 1,000 Coins Golden Gift Box progress, unlocked Scenic Themes, and free boosters (Undos, Smart Hints, and Extra Tubes).
  • 7-Day Sign-In Calendar streak, Daily Challenge progress, 7-day gameplay win statistics, and audio/vibration preferences.

Note: Your player display name and gameplay progress are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to any external account server owned by us. You can edit your profile name or reset all saved game data at any time inside the App's Settings screen ("Reset Game Progress").

B. Device & Advertising Data (Google AdMob SDK)

Our App integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display Banner Ads, Interstitial Ads, and optional Rewarded Video Ads (such as 2X Double Coins, 2X Calendar Bonus, Lucky Spin, and Free Boosters). When ads are requested or displayed, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set ID.
  • Coarse/approximate IP-based location, device model, operating system version, screen dimensions, and mobile network information.
  • Ad impression, click, and video completion interaction events for ad delivery, frequency capping, analytics, and fraud prevention.

2. App Permissions We Request

Water Sort Master requests only the minimum Android permissions required to run the game:

  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load and display Google AdMob advertisements when your device is online. The core Water Sort puzzle gameplay works offline.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used to provide gentle haptic vibration feedback when tapping bottles or completing levels (can be toggled ON or OFF anytime in Game Settings).

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, SMS, call logs, or precise GPS location.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App and its integrated SDKs is used strictly to:

  • Operate the Water Sort puzzle engine, save your levels, and recover your in-progress bottles automatically.
  • Personalize your in-app VIP Player Profile card and track your 7-day puzzle statistics graph.
  • Serve Banner, Interstitial, and user-initiated Rewarded Video advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Measure ad performance, prevent invalid traffic or fraud, and maintain app stability.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us at the email address below and we will promptly delete any such information.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion Controls (GDPR / CCPA)

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Data Reset: Open Game Settings ⚙️ → Reset Progress inside the App to immediately erase all locally stored levels, coins, profile name, and statistics.
  • Android System Storage Clear / Uninstall: You can clear the App's storage or uninstall the App from your Android device settings to permanently remove all local data.
  • Advertising ID Reset / Opt-Out: On your Android phone, open Settings → Google → All services → Ads to reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising.
